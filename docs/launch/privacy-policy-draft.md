# Kaira Privacy Policy

**Draft of 14 November 2026**

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

Kaira is operated by `[Company name]`, registered address `[registered address]`. We are the data controller for personal data processed through Kaira, as that term is used in the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021.

Questions about your data go to `[data protection contact]` or `[contact email]`.

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use Kaira, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It applies to the Kaira app at https://kaira-jolezya-s-projects.vercel.app and any later Kaira domain.

It does not cover what buyers and sellers do with each other's details after they agree a deal. Each user is responsible for their own use of another person's data.

## 3. The data we collect

**When you create an account**
- Your name, email address and a password. The password is stored only as a hash; we cannot read it.
- Your town, so buyers nearby see your listings first.
- If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture, as provided by Google.

**When you list something**
- Photos, title, description, category, price, condition and location of the item.
- Your phone number, only if you choose to add it. It is optional. If you add it, it is shown on the listing.
- Delivery options you select.

**When you use Kaira**
- Messages and offers you send and receive.
- Items and searches you save, and sellers you follow.
- Listings you view, and the number of views on your own listings.
- Reports you make about listings or users.
- Notification preferences and your choice of light or dark mode.
- Technical data: your browser type, device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and error logs when something goes wrong.

**Stored on your device**
Kaira stores some data in your browser so it works offline: a copy of the app, listings you have viewed, drafts, saved items and your settings. Until the shared backend is switched on, this is the only place your data lives.

## 4. Why we use your data

Under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, we need a lawful basis for each use. These are ours.

| What we do | Why | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create and run your account | So you can sign in, list and message | Performance of our contract with you |
| Show your listings to buyers, including your town and, if added, your phone number | That is what a marketplace does | Performance of our contract |
| Deliver messages and offers between users | So deals can be agreed | Performance of our contract |
| Send a confirmation email and account emails | Security and service | Performance of our contract |
| Send tips and offers by email or notification | To help you sell | Your consent, which you can withdraw in your profile at any time |
| Review reports, detect fraud and duplicate listings | To keep Kaira safe | Our legitimate interest in a trustworthy marketplace, and legal obligation where the law requires action |
| Count views and produce seller analytics | To show sellers how listings perform | Our legitimate interest, and the contract for paid plans that include analytics |
| Fix errors and improve Kaira | To keep the service working | Our legitimate interest |
| Keep records of payments for Kaira's fees | Tax and accounting | Legal obligation |

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising by other companies.

## 5. Who sees your data

**Other users.** Your name, town, profile picture, listings and any phone number you add to a listing are visible to everyone who uses Kaira. Your email address is never shown to other users. Your messages are visible only to you and the person you are chatting with.

**Service providers.** We use companies that process data on our behalf and only on our instructions:
- Supabase, which hosts our database, authentication and photo storage, in a data centre in the European Union.
- Vercel, which hosts the Kaira app.
- Google, if you choose Google sign-in.
- An email delivery provider for account emails.
- A mobile money or payment provider when you pay for a Kaira plan or Boost. Kaira never handles the payment between buyers and sellers, and we never see your mobile money PIN.

**Authorities.** We disclose data where Zambian law requires it, for example to the police or a court, or to the Zambia Information and Communications Technology Authority or the Data Protection Commissioner on a lawful request.

**A new owner.** If Kaira is sold or merged, your data may pass to the new owner under this same policy. We would tell you first.

Our hosting providers store data in the European Union and may process it in other countries. The Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 restricts transferring personal data outside Zambia. ## 7. Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords and access rules on the database so that each user can only read and change their own data. Photos are stored in a protected storage bucket. Access to the administration tools is limited to named staff. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it. If we learn of a breach that puts you at risk, we will notify you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the Act requires.

## 8. How long we keep your data

- Account data: for as long as your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of a deletion request, except where we must keep it longer.
- Listings: visible until they expire or you remove them, then kept for 12 months in case of a dispute, then deleted.
- Messages: for as long as both accounts are open, then deleted within 30 days of either account being deleted.
- Reports and records of removed content: 24 months, to deal with repeat abuse and legal requests.
- Payment records for Kaira's fees: as required by Zambian tax law, which the lawyer should confirm (we have assumed 6 years).
- Technical logs: 90 days.

## 9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 you can:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** data that is wrong. Most of your profile you can edit yourself.
- **Delete** your account and data, subject to the retention rules above.
- **Object** to processing based on our legitimate interests.
- **Withdraw consent** to marketing messages at any time, in your profile under notification settings or by emailing us.
- **Receive a copy** of the data you have given us in a usable format.
- **Complain** to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if you believe we have handled your data unlawfully. We would ask you to contact us first so we can try to put it right.

To use any of these rights, email `[data protection contact]`. We will respond within the time the Act allows and never later than 30 days.

## 10. Children

Kaira is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and we will remove it.

## 11. Cookies and local storage

Kaira does not use advertising cookies. It uses local storage and a service worker in your browser to keep you signed in, remember your settings, and let the app open offline. Clearing your browser data removes this. In single-device mode, before the shared backend is live, clearing browser data also removes your Kaira account and listings on that device, because that is the only place they exist.

## 12. Electronic communications

Account emails and notifications are sent electronically. Under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, electronic messages and records have the same standing as paper ones. Marketing messages are sent only with your consent and every one includes a way to stop them.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is material we will tell you in Kaira or by email at least 14 days before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
