# Kaira FAQ

Twelve questions testers actually ask.

---

**1. Is Kaira free?**

Yes. Browsing is free. Listing is free on the Mahala plan, which gives you a complete listing: up to 8 photos, title, price, location, normal search placement, buyer chat, and 30 days live. You can sell everything you own without paying a Kwacha.

Paid options exist only for extra visibility. Premium is K30 per listing (K80 for vehicles, property and business) and Gold is K75 (K200 for vehicles, property and business). A Boost lifts any listing to the top of search for 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days, from K15. All prices include 16% VAT. During testing none of this is charged.

---

**2. How do I pay the seller? Does Kaira take a cut?**

Kaira never handles the payment between buyer and seller, and takes no commission. You agree the price in chat and settle the way you both prefer: MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, bank transfer, or cash when you meet. For anything valuable, meet in a public place and check the item before you pay.

---

**3. Where is the app in the App Store or Play Store?**

It is not there, and does not need to be. Open https://kaira-jolezya-s-projects.vercel.app in your browser and add it to your home screen. On iPhone, use Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the three dots in Chrome, then Add to Home screen. It then opens full screen with its own icon, like any app. See the install guide for every phone.

---

**4. Do I need an account to look around?**

No. You can browse and search without signing in. You need an account to save items, message a seller, or list something. Signing up takes your name, email, a password and your town. You can also tap the Google button.

---

**5. I signed up on my phone. Why is nothing there on my laptop?**

During testing Kaira runs in single-device mode. Your account, listings and messages live on the device you signed up on. When the shared backend is switched on, you sign up once more and your account then works on every device. We will tell you when that happens.

Until then, please do not clear your browser data on that phone. In single-device mode that deletes your Kaira account and listings.

---

**6. How many photos can I add, and how long does a listing stay up?**

Mahala: up to 8 photos, live for 30 days.
Premium: up to 15 photos, live for 45 days.
Gold: up to 25 photos, live for 60 days.

Tap the star on a photo to make it the cover. The cover is what buyers see in search.

---

**7. What is the difference between Premium, Gold and a Boost?**

Premium puts your listing higher in search, adds a Premium badge, gives it featured placement and more exposure in recommendations, and keeps it live for 45 days.

Gold does everything Premium does and adds top placement, a Gold badge, homepage exposure and featured placement in the category hub, live for 60 days.

A Boost is separate. It lifts an existing listing to the very top for a set time without changing its plan: 24 hours (K15, or K30 for vehicles, property and business), 3 days (K30 or K60) or 7 days (K45 or K100). You can add a Boost or upgrade the plan from the listing page after publishing. No need to start again.

---

**8. How do I make an offer?**

Open the listing and tap Message. In the chat, tap the plus button next to the message box and choose Make offer. Type your amount and send it. The seller sees it in the chat and can accept or reply with their own number.

---

**9. Is my phone number shown to everyone?**

Only if you add it. The phone number field in the Sell flow is optional. Leave it blank and buyers reach you through chat inside Kaira. Add it and it shows on your listing.

---

**10. I think a listing is a scam. What do I do?**

Open the listing, scroll down past the map, tap **Report this listing**, and choose a reason. Every report is read by a person. Do not send money to anyone you have not met, do not pay a "deposit" to reserve an item, and be careful of prices far below what similar items sell for.

---

**11. How do I edit or remove a listing?**

Go to your profile. Your active listings are there. Tap a listing to edit it, or use the delete button to remove it. Drafts you have not published sit in the same place. To remove your whole account, email [reply address] and we delete it.

---

**12. What are the monthly plans for?**

Most people never need one. Monthly plans are for people who list a lot.

Free: up to 10 active listings, with everything Mahala offers, and pay-as-you-go Premium, Gold and Boost.
Plus (K150 a month): up to 25 active listings, Premium placement included on every everyday listing, half-price Premium on vehicles, property and business, two 3-day Boosts a month, a Plus badge and seller analytics.
Pro (K950 a month): up to 40 active listings in any category, Premium placement on every listing including big-ticket ones, 5 rotating Gold slots, six 3-day Boosts a month, a business profile with logo and 3 staff seats, bulk upload and a lead inbox.
Corporate: for dealerships, developers and retailers at scale. Unlimited listings, Gold placement across the marketplace, API integration and a dedicated account manager. Price on request.

Prices include 16% VAT. When paid plans go live, you pay with MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or Zamtel Kwacha.
