# Install Kaira on your phone

Kaira is not in the App Store or Play Store. It does not need to be. Open it once in your browser, add it to your home screen, and it opens full screen like any other app, with its own icon. It takes about ten seconds.

The address is https://kaira-jolezya-s-projects.vercel.app

---

## iPhone and iPad

Use **Safari**. Other browsers on iPhone cannot add Kaira to the home screen.

1. Open https://kaira-jolezya-s-projects.vercel.app in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button. It is the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen on iPhone and at the top on iPad.
3. Scroll down the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. The name shows as **Kaira**. Tap **Add** in the top right corner.

Kaira now sits on your home screen. Open it from there, not from Safari.

If you do not see Add to Home Screen, scroll further down the share list. On older versions of iOS it is near the bottom.

---

## Android

### Chrome

1. Open https://kaira-jolezya-s-projects.vercel.app in Chrome.
2. Tap the **three dots** in the top right corner.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**. On some phones this says **Install app** instead. Both do the same thing.
4. Tap **Install** or **Add**.

Some phones show a banner at the bottom that says "Add Kaira to Home screen". Tapping that works too.

### Samsung Internet

1. Open the address in Samsung Internet.
2. Tap the **three lines** (Menu) in the bottom right corner.
3. Tap **Add page to**.
4. Tap **Home screen**, then **Add**.

### Other Android browsers

Most have an **Add to Home screen** or **Install** option in their menu. If yours does not, open the address in Chrome and follow the Chrome steps.

---

## Laptop or desktop

Chrome and Edge show a small install icon at the right end of the address bar. Click it, then click **Install**. Kaira opens in its own window.

## What works offline

Kaira keeps a copy of itself on your phone, so it opens even with no signal.

**Works with no connection**
- Opening Kaira and moving between screens.
- Listings you have already looked at, including their photos.
- Your saved items and saved searches.
- Your drafts. If you lose signal halfway through a listing, tap Save draft and finish later.
- Light and dark mode, and your settings.

**Needs a connection**
- Signing in or signing up.
- Seeing new listings from other people.
- Sending and receiving messages and offers.
- Publishing a listing so other people can see it.
- Photos you have not opened before.

## Two things to know during testing

**One phone for now.** Until the shared backend is switched on, Kaira runs in single-device mode. Your account, listings and messages live on the phone you signed up on. They do not appear on another device. When the backend goes live you will sign up once more and the account will then work everywhere.

**Do not clear browser data.** In single-device mode, clearing Safari or Chrome data deletes your Kaira account and listings on that phone. Once the backend is live this no longer matters.
